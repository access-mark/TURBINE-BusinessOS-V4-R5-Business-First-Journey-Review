**Date:** 17 November 2026  
**Decision:** GO for founder visual/content review; NO-GO for public indexable release.  
**Change classification:** Guided visitor experience and plain-English story; no redesign of Original V4.

## What was built

The visitor starts with how they run their business rather than a default occupation. Five selectable pathways lead to a practical five-step working day: new business, working for oneself, small team, growing business, and larger organisation. There is a second choice for the daily issue to improve (customers, work, money, or people), followed by relevant capability and adoption links. The occupation directory remains an optional route.

The homepage now places this starting point directly after the hero. The floating guide's old self-employed-to-gardener assumption is removed on all pages. Context-specific conclusions replace the repeated standard ending across the site. The original V4-R4 content scope, logos and screenshots remain intact.
