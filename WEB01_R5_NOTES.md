# WEB-01 V4-R5 | Business-first narrative and guide

Status: REVIEW ONLY. No public repository modified.

## What's new
- Start with business situation rather than occupation. No gardener or other role assumed for sole proprietors.
- Five starting points: starting a business, working alone, small team, growing business, larger organisation.
- Standalone `my-business.html` with five illustrated, stage-specific customer-to-cash journeys.
- Optional occupation search remains available for 780 roles; 29 curated static role stories retained.
- Guided focus by customers, work, money and people/decisions; capability detail remains available.
- Sitewide floating guidance routes to business situations rather than stereotypes.
- Clear, contextual endings on content pages, with the next sensible step.
- R4 CSS/nav and source assets preserved, plus isolated R5 component styles; no !important declarations.

## Important
Illustrative journeys describe intended workflows, not production promises. The site remains NOINDEX for preview. Enquiry forms have no approved delivery destination. Founder acceptance, independent product claims, security, lead capture, consent and full live browser qualification are required before public indexable release.
